# Play Console: Content rating (IARC questionnaire)

Play Console → App content → Content rating → Start questionnaire.
Source: [Content rating requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859655)
and [Content ratings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898843).

Google doesn't publish the question list; it depends on the category you pick
and changes over time. The answers below cover every topic the IARC
questionnaire asks about. **Read each question on screen and answer from the
facts column, not by position.** If a question isn't covered here, answer from
the facts and add it to this file.

## Contact and category

| Field | Answer |
|---|---|
| Email for IARC certificates | support@academe.cc |
| Category | **Reference, News, or Educational** ("apps whose main purpose is to inform or educate"). Not "Game": XP and streaks are motivation, not a game. Not "Social": students can't talk to each other |

## Expected result

Everyone / 3+ style ratings in most regions (PEGI 3, ESRB Everyone, USK 0).
The "Users interact" descriptor should not appear; "In-app purchases" will.
Because Pebby's output is generative, some regions may add "Users interact"
or a higher rating if the AI question is answered Yes. That's fine: don't
change answers to get a lower rating.

## When to redo the questionnaire

Any of: students can message each other or share decks publicly, ads are added,
lessons on reproductive health or drugs ship with images, a browser or open web
search is added, loot-box style rewards. Resubmitting is free and the rating
updates in a few hours.
