# Play Console: Store listing

Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing. Language: English
(India) `en-IN` as default. Character counts checked with Python on
2026-09-25 (Play counts characters, not bytes).

Rules that shaped the copy ([Metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842)):
no emoji, no ALL CAPS (except the brand), no "best", "#1", "free" or price
promotions in the title, icon or developer name (the policy scopes these to
those three fields; upper-case section headings in the description are allowed), no competitor names, no claims we can't back. Keep the copy
honest about today's content: lessons exist for a few chapters so far, so the
listing describes how lessons work, not how many there are.

## Full description (3157 / 4000)

```
ACADEMe is a study app for Indian school students in Class 6 to 12, CBSE and ICSE. Meet Pebby, your AI tutor: ask a doubt, scan your homework, learn a chapter in short swipe cards, and plan for your exams, in the language you think in.

PEBBY, YOUR AI TUTOR
• Explain: Pebby teaches the idea step by step, with an everyday Indian example, then checks you understood.
• Solve: stuck on a question? Pebby gives you a hint first and lets you take the next step, so you learn how to do it, not just the answer.
• Quiz me: Pebby asks one question at a time at your class level and tells you why an answer is right or wrong.
• Knows your syllabus: your class and board decide what Pebby teaches.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• Pebby answers in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Bengali.
• Science and exam terms stay in English in brackets, so you're ready for the paper.
• Switch language any time from Me.

SCAN YOUR HOMEWORK
• Solve homework: take a photo of the question and Pebby helps you solve it, hint first.
• Check my answer: photograph your written answer and get marks the CBSE or ICSE way, what you missed, and how to get full marks.
• Notes to a folder: photograph your notes (up to 10 pages) and turn them into a swipe lesson or keep them as text.
• We don't store your photos. Only the text we read is kept.

LEARN BY SWIPING
• Lessons are short card decks: concepts, tables, worked examples you reveal one step at a time, and a summary.
• A quick check every few cards, with "here's why" when you get one wrong.
• Keep any card for revision. Every question you miss is kept for you automatically.
• Revision brings kept cards back at the right time: Didn't know, Almost, or Knew it.
• Chapter tests with a report by lesson.

PLAN FOR YOUR EXAMS
• Make a folder for a test or exam, add the chapters and a date.
• ACADEMe turns the date into a day-by-day plan, a checklist and revision days.
• Today on Home shows everything due today across your folders.
• Gentle reminders: an evening study nudge at a time you choose, and reminders 3 days before, the day before and the morning of your exam.

XP THAT MEANS SOMETHING
• Earn XP for right answers, not for ticking boxes.

FAIR AND SAFE FOR STUDENTS
• No ads. No paywall while you set up.
• Free every day, with daily limits on AI features. ACADEMe Pro removes the limits.
• We never sell your data. No location, contacts or microphone access.
• Report any AI answer that looks wrong or harmful, right in the chat.
• Delete your account from inside the app at any time.

ACADEMe PRO (OPTIONAL)
• No daily limits on Pebby, Scan and Check my answer, plus swipe lessons made from your notes.
• ₹200 a month, with your first month at ₹100 where the offer is available, or ₹1,999 a year.
• Billed through Google Play. Renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in Google Play > Payments and subscriptions.

Pebby's answers are generated by AI and can be wrong. Check important answers with your textbook or teacher.

Students under 18 should use ACADEMe with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Privacy policy: https://academe.cc/privacy
Terms of use: https://academe.cc/terms
Help: support@academe.cc
```

Before publishing, check the Pro bullet against the live paywall: prices come
from Play Console, and the ₹100 first month must be set up as an introductory
offer on the monthly base plan (see `subscriptions.md`). If the free limits
change (`ACADEME_FREE_LIMITS`, today 10 Pebby questions, 3 scans and 1 answer
check a day, lessons from notes Pro only), the copy still holds because it
names no numbers.
