What's wrong with the website now

It sells a different product

"Turn notes, PDFs, lectures and slides into AI practice". The app is a Class 6–12 CBSE/ICSE/ISC app with syllabus lessons, Pebby, Scan and folders. There's no PDF or slide upload in the app.

The phone screens are fake

All nine phone screens are drawn by scripts/build-screens.mjs: study modes, uploads, a chat with "Mee", a waitlist. None exist in the app.

Wrong mascot and colours

"Mee" instead of Pebby. Primary #5B6CFF instead of the app's #564CF1, and a different font.

Delete account is broken

The form posts to the old backend academe-api-production.up.railway.app, which returns 404. Google Play requires this page to work.

Old privacy policy

From August 2026. It doesn't cover Pebby's AI, Scan, Sarvam, RevenueCat, Resend or the Families ages. The accurate one is on api.academe.cc.

academe.cc fails half the time

Its DNS has two A records: Vercel and Namecheap parking (162.255.119.53). Browsers that hit the parking IP get a certificate error. www works.

Promises that aren't live

An App Store badge (no iOS app yet), a waitlist form link, "Start for free, no credit card" with nothing to download, and a demo video on an outside video site.

Other app names

The design/ folder names competitors and other apps (a scraped design system, competitive UI notes). They have to go.

What's good and stays

The ACADEMe cube loader, the 3D phone scroll story, the big ACADEMe footer wordmark, smooth scrolling, the /design brand docs.

Screen swaps: hidden in the spins, timed to the text

The screens follow the text fades, not the waypoint labels. Two swaps happen while the phone is edge-on: lesson → courses at 0.32, and check → revision at 0.762 (checked numerically on both the desktop and phone paths). The rest happen while the headline crossfades: home → lesson 0.12, courses → askme 0.465, askme → scan 0.615, scan → check 0.69, revision → folder 0.875, folder → landscape 0.945.

The text fades moved slightly so the Lessons card gets time on screen: Lessons out at 0.41, Pebby in at 0.48 and out at 0.59, Scan in at 0.62 and out at 0.73.
