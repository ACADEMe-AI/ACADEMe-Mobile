Your subjects

Home and Study currently list every subject the board offers, and the Home tiles can't be tapped. The plan is to let each student pick their own subjects once, show only those, and turn every tile into a way into that subject's lessons.

1 · Today Problem

A Class 12 CBSE Science student sees all 14 senior subjects, from Accountancy to Psychology. The tiles are plain labels, and most of them lead to chapters marked "Coming soon".

2 · Pick subjects in setup New step 5

This is one more step in the existing setup sheet, after the board. Class 6–10 comes with every subject already selected, so most students just tap Finish. Class 11–12 picks a stream first, which fills in the usual subjects, then adds any optional ones. English is always included.

3 · Home tiles Tappable

Home shows only the student's subjects. Each tile is a keycap with an icon, a progress bar and "N of M lessons". Subjects with no lessons yet show a small "Soon" badge and still open, because their chapters and the plan are already visible. The section has an Edit link.

4 · Tap a tile → Study Filtered

Tapping a tile opens Study → Courses with that subject selected. The subject chips at the top list only the student's subjects. Chapters without lessons stay greyed out as "Coming soon", as they are today.

5 · Change later Me

Me → Class and board gets a "Subjects" row that opens the same picker. Changing class or board resets the picks to that syllabus's defaults and says so.

8 · Filtering in Study → Courses New

Today Courses has subject chips and All / In progress / Done pills. With every chapter of the syllabus listed (hundreds for Class 11–12), students need to narrow things down by board-exam relevance, lessons that are ready, revision that's due, and search.

9 · What gets built

Server

  • New profile field subjects (list of subject codes). Migration 0023 adds a text[] column.
  • PATCH /me/profile accepts subjects and checks every code against that class and board.
  • GET /catalog/streams?class=&board= returns the Class 11–12 streams (Science PCM, Science PCB, Commerce, Humanities) with their default and optional subjects.
  • Changing class or board clears the picks, and the app asks again.
  • GET /study/decks adds lesson counts per subject (planned and ready) for the tiles. The change is additive only.

App

  • A "Your subjects" step in the setup sheet (+25 XP like the other steps), and a subjects row in Me → Class and board.
  • SubjectGrid becomes tappable keycap tiles with an icon, progress and a Soon badge, and opens Study filtered to that subject.
  • Study's subject chips, Add chapters and the Today plan use only the picked subjects.
  • Existing students have no picks, so the app treats that as "all subjects" and shows a one-time nudge: "Pick your subjects".

Not in this change

  • Per-subject reminders or goals.
  • State boards.
  • A "notify me when lessons arrive" button for Soon subjects. It could come later.
