Study smarter, in your own language.

ACADEMe is a study app for Class 6 to 12, CBSE and ICSE. Pebby, your AI tutor, explains, solves and quizzes. Lessons are short cards you swipe through, with a quick check every few cards.

Get it on Google Play

Pebby, your tutor

Ask anything from your syllabus. Pebby explains step by step, gives a hint before the answer, and quizzes you when you're ready.

Your language

Pebby answers in English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil or Bengali. Switch any time.

Learn by swiping

Lessons are card decks with a quick check every few cards and a "here's why" whenever you get one wrong.

Knows your syllabus

Your class and board decide the subjects, the chapters and what Pebby teaches.

Scan your homework

Take a photo to get a hint, check your answer against the marking scheme, or turn your notes into a lesson.

Plan for your exams

Folders turn an exam date into a day-by-day plan, a checklist and gentle reminders. What you miss comes back in revision.

Built for students

No ads. No paywall while you set up. Your data is never sold. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time. Read our privacy policy.
