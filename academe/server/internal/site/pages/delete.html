Delete your ACADEMe account

You can delete your ACADEMe account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ACADEMe and tap Me.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete my account.
  3. Choose a reason if you like, type DELETE and confirm.

You're logged out straight away. After 30 days everything linked to your account is erased for good: your name, email, password, profile (language, birth year, class, board), study progress and XP, folders, notes and to-dos, chats with Pebby, scans and their results, reports, and subscription records on our server. If you log in again within those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it in the Google Play app under Payments and subscriptions.

What we keep

We keep the web deletion request itself (the email address, the time and your IP address) for up to one year to show we acted on it and to stop abuse. Google Play and RevenueCat keep records of purchases as payment laws and their own policies require.
