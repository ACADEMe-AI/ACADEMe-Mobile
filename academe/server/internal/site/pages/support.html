Support

Something not working, an answer that looks wrong, or a question about your data? We're here to help.

Email us

support@academe.cc. Please write from the email address you signed up with, so we can find your account. We aim to reply within 2 working days.

Common requests

  • Forgot your password: on the log-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a code.
  • Wrong or harmful answer from Pebby: tap Report under the answer. You can also email us.
  • Change your class, board or language: Me > Class and board, or Me > App language.
  • Cancel ACADEMe Pro: Google Play app > Payments and subscriptions > Subscriptions > ACADEMe.
  • Get a copy of your data: email us and we'll send it to the address on your account.
  • Delete your account: see Delete your account.
