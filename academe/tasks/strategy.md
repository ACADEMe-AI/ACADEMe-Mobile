# ACADEMe — strategy

Why ACADEMe exists, who it is for, how it wins and how it earns. The build plan,
every task and everything that needs the user are in `tasks/roadmap.md`.

Last updated 2026-09-25.

---

## 1. Vision and mission

- **Vision:** every Indian school student has a patient tutor who knows their
  exact syllabus and speaks their language.
- **Mission:** turn the board syllabus into short lessons a student finishes on
  a phone, and give them Pebby to explain, solve, quiz and mark, so they walk
  into every test prepared. Free at the core, fair when paid, safe for minors.

## 2. The problem

Indian students in Class 6–12 (CBSE, ICSE, and ISC for 11–12) study for
high-stakes board exams on budget Android phones, often in a language other
than English.

| Pain | Today |
|---|---|
| The syllabus is the exam, but most study apps are generic | they teach topics, not "Class 10 CBSE, chapter 9, lesson 2" |
| Homework help ends at the answer | students copy; they don't learn how marks are awarded |
| English-first products | Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and Bengali speakers get translated menus, not teaching |
| Long videos and long notes | don't fit a 10-minute bus ride or low data |
| No plan | tests arrive and revision is guesswork |
| Paywalls in onboarding, metered "generations" | families pay before seeing value |

## 5. Product pillars

1. **Pebby tutor (ASKMe)** — explain, solve step by step, quiz; linked from every card and every scan.
2. **Swipe lessons from the syllabus (Courses)** — generated by the pipeline, reviewed by a model and a teacher, text first; diagrams, listen and video cards later.
3. **Scan with board-style marking** — Solve, Check my answer, Notes to a folder, Ask about a photo.
4. **Folders and planner** — a name and a date become a plan, a Today checklist and reminders.
5. **Revision** — every kept card and missed question in one spaced queue; chapter tests by lesson.

Everything else (streaks, levels, weekly report, mock exams) serves these five.

## 6. How we differ from competitors

Described generically; no names.

| Other study apps | ACADEMe |
|---|---|
| Built for a global or US audience, adapted to India | built for CBSE / ICSE / ISC 6–12 from day one |
| A chat box is the product | the syllabus is the product; Pebby is the tutor inside it |
| Video lectures or long notes | 5–8 minute card decks with checks every few cards |
| Homework answers | hint-first solving and marking against the board scheme |
| Peer-uploaded notes feeds | curated, reviewed lessons; no social surface for minors |
| Paywall during onboarding, credits on every tool | free core, one simple Pro, limits only on the costly AI |
| English UI, translated late | five Indian languages planned from the start, via Sarvam |

## 7. Business model and pricing

| | Free | ACADEMe Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Lessons, revision, folders, reminders | all | all |
| ASKMe (Pebby) | 10 messages a day | unlimited |
| Scan reads | 3 a day | unlimited |
| Check my answer | 1 a day | unlimited |
| Lesson from your notes | — (notes kept free) | yes |
| Coming: mock exams, weekly parent report | — | yes |
| Price | ₹0 | **₹100 first month, then ₹200/month**, or **₹1,999/year** (≈ ₹167/month, save 17%) |

Sold as one Play subscription (`academe_pro`) through RevenueCat. Limits reset
at IST midnight; the server enforces them.

### Unit economics (estimates, to be measured)

Sarvam list price: ₹29.28 per 1M input tokens, ₹73.2 per 1M output tokens.

| Item | Assumption | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| One ASKMe message | ~3k input (prompt + last 20 messages), ~500 output, reasoning off | ≈ ₹0.10–0.15 |
| A Free student at the cap | 10 messages × 30 days | ≈ ₹40/month worst case; typical use far lower |
| A heavy Pro student | 30 messages/day | ≈ ₹110/month |
| One lesson generated | ~12k input + 18k output (writer + reviewer) | ≈ ₹1.7–2.2, one-off |
| All 5,777 lessons, one language | one pass + retries | ≈ ₹10,000–13,000, one-off |
| Scan (Document AI) and marking | not yet measured | measure in the first month |
| Hosting | Railway Pro, usage ≈ $7 inside the $20 fee | ≈ ₹1,700/month fixed |

Revenue per subscriber (Play prices include 18% GST; Play keeps 15% of
subscriptions): monthly ₹200 → ≈ ₹144 net; first month ₹100 → ≈ ₹72;
annual ₹1,999 → ≈ ₹1,440 (≈ ₹120/month).

**Decisions that follow:** keep reasoning off for chat; watch cost per active
Free user weekly and tune `ACADEME_FREE_LIMITS` (no release needed); lessons
are a one-off asset, so generating them is the best spend we have.

## 8. Go-to-market (India)

1. **Shipaton launch, 30 Sep 2026** — the rebuild ships with RevenueCat Pro; judges get promotional Pro.
2. **Play update to the existing listing** — 2.0.0 replaces the old ACADEMe, India only, staged rollout.
3. **Schools without a school product** — teacher talks and posters in a few pilot schools per city; a teacher recommends, the student installs. No teacher dashboard.
4. **Sharing in messaging apps** — folder share links, a weekly progress card for parents, "I got 5/5 on this chapter" cards. Every share opens the app or the Play listing.
5. **Exam seasons** — push before half-yearlies (Sep–Dec) and boards (Feb–Mar): board-date folders, Class 10 and 12 lessons generated first, mock exams before February.
6. **Language-first** — listings, emails and Pebby in Hindi, Telugu, Tamil and Bengali as Stage 2 lands; target regions where English-first products are weakest.
7. **Price as the message** — "₹100 for your first month" and "every lesson free" in the listing (drop the price from the listing if Play review objects).

## 9. Metrics

**North star: daily active learners** — students who finish at least one card
deck, revision session, Check or ASKMe answer in a day.

| Metric | Target to start with |
|---|---|
| Lessons completed per learner per week | 5+ |
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 40% / 20% / 10% |
| Setup checklist finished | 70% of sign-ups |
| Free → Pro conversion | 3% of monthly actives in 90 days |
| Pro monthly churn | < 10% |
| Marking accuracy (Check my answer vs a teacher) | within 1 mark on 80% of answers, sampled weekly |
| Lesson quality | < 1 report per 1,000 lesson views; teacher approval ≥ 90% on spot checks |
| AI safety | every harmful report handled the same day |
| Cost per active Free user | tracked weekly against §7 |

Instrumentation is a gap: there is no analytics or crash reporting yet
(roadmap §4 "Not yet"). Pick a privacy-safe, Families-approved option before
launch or compute these from the server tables.

## 10. Content strategy

- **Source of truth:** the board syllabus files (CBSE curriculum and NCERT books; CISCE syllabuses and publishers' books), refreshed every session.
- **AI pipeline:** Sarvam writes and reviews each lesson; code enforces the card rules; drafts never reach students.
- **Human review:** a teacher spot-checks every batch (drafts first) and the syllabus checks listed in the roadmap.
- **Order:** Class 10 and 12 first (board years), then 9 and 11, then 6–8.
- **Languages later:** English first (Hindi and Sanskrit lessons in Hindi); translated and native-reviewed lessons in Stage 2, exam terms kept in English.
- **Refresh:** yearly syllabus roll (ICSE 10 and ISC 12 move to the next exam year); CI flags any lesson that drifts from the syllabus.

## 11. Compliance and trust

- **DPDP:** every user is a child in law. Verifiable parental consent, notice, consent records, retention and breach rules apply from **13 May 2027**; design and build them in the 90-day plan, with a lawyer.
- **Play Families:** target ages 9–12, 13–15, 16–17; no ads, no advertising ID, only Families-suitable SDKs; Data safety answers written.
- **Generative AI:** Report on every Pebby answer, weekly review; moderation filter and helpline answer before scale.
- **Data:** photos never stored; hosting in Singapore disclosed; account deletion in the app and on the web; processors (Sarvam, Railway, Resend, RevenueCat) under DPAs.
- **No ads, ever.** Pro pays for the product.

## 12. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Sarvam dependency and credits** (credits ran out on launch week; one vendor for chat, OCR and lessons) | keep a funded balance with an alert; generation stops cleanly on 402 and resumes; chat and scan fail with a clear message; keep a second model option open (D2) |
| **Content accuracy** | reviewer model + code checks + teacher spot-checks; Report on answers; approved-only serving |
| **Board syllabus changes** (new CBSE 9 books, ICSE year rolls) | syllabus as data with sources; CI detects drift; regenerate by chapter |
| **Play review** (Families, subscriptions, AI policy, listing price claims) | `docs/play/` answers, paywall per policy, reviewer account, staged rollout |
| **Costs** (Free users at the cap, Scan unmeasured) | server-side limits tunable by env; weekly cost per active user |
| **Minors' data and DPDP** | no ads, minimal data, consent flow planned before May 2027, lawyer review |
| **Single region, single instance** (Singapore, in-memory limiters) | Pro backups and PITR, limiters to Postgres before scaling, Mumbai path documented |
| **Small team, many workstreams** | one roadmap, reports per workstream, tests and UI journeys as the gate |
