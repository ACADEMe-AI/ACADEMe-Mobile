# Cleanup: competitor material removed, design previews merged

## 1. Competitor material removed

Where it came from: everything in `assets/academe/` except our logo, Pebby and
the Khagwal key was copied from an old reference folder in the previous app. That
folder had been extracted from a competitor's app (the leading study app); its
logo, badges, exam art and feature illustrations were in there. Every file in it
went, except those listed under Kept.

### Removed (121 files)

| Folder | Files | What |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/academe/raster/` | 60 | every file except `academe_cube.png` and `logo_mark.png` |
| `assets/academe/vector/` | 42 | the whole folder: the competitor's logo and badges, streak and XP medals, laurels, sprites |
| `assets/academe/lottie/` | 12 | the whole folder, including the sign-up confetti; see Sign-up confetti below |
| `assets/academe/audio/` | 4 | answer and streak sounds. They came from the same extraction and nothing of ours is in their metadata (only an ffmpeg encoder tag), so I treated them as the competitor's. Nothing played them. |
| `assets/academe/dream_college/` | 2 | the competitor's "dream college" data |
| `screenshots/` | 49 | captures of the old openme app, which was a replica of a competitor |

Nothing was permanently deleted. The files are in the scratchpad at
`scratchpad/removed/`.

### Kept (ours)

- **Logo:** `raster/logo_mark.png` (used by the splash) and `raster/academe_cube.png` (used by `BrandMark`). I looked at both: they are the ACADEMe cube.
- **Pebby:** `mascot/animations/pebby.riv`, generated by `design/mascot/rive/`, plus `mascot/source/` and `catalog.json`.
- **Illustrations:** `illustrations/`, the Khagwal key (CC0).
- **Fonts:** `assets/fonts/`.
- **Preview images:** all images in `design/preview/`.

### Sign-up confetti

The sign-up screen's "done" confetti was a Lottie file that came from the
competitor. It is now our own `Burst` from `lib/ui/core/ui/celebration.dart`.

- **What it looks like:** 18 dots in the app colours, 320 px across, over 900 ms.
- **How it's built:** a `CustomPaint` with no `saveLayer` and no `Opacity`. It respects reduce-motion.
- **Code change:** in `lib/ui/auth/widgets/sign_up_screen.dart`, `_Confetti` now returns the Burst, and the `lottie` import is gone.

- **Removed packages:** `lottie` (nothing used it any more) and `audioplayers` (nothing ever used it).
